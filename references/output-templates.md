# Output templates

Use only the tables relevant to the selected mode. Empty or unknown fields must
be marked `Unknown`, `Not reported`, or `Requires verification`; never infer a
value merely to complete a table.

## Literature matrix

| ID | Citation | Research question | Theory/framework | Unit, context, sample/data | Design/method | Measures or analysis | Main finding | Boundary/limitation | Relevance | Evidence status |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|

`Evidence status` should indicate whether the full source was inspected,
abstract-only, secondary-report-only, or still unverified.

## Concept and relationship map

| Concept/construct | Working definition | Theoretical origin | Role or level | Indicator/measure/coding | Proposed relation or mechanism | Evidence | Open problem |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|

For a causal model, add direction, timing, confounders, mediators, moderators,
and the unit of analysis. For qualitative or theoretical work, replace those
columns with process stages, propositions, conditions, and rival explanations.

## Contradiction map

| Claim or relationship | Supporting evidence | Contrary or null evidence | What differs across studies | Plausible explanation | Decisive test or next question | Current judgment |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|

`Current judgment` should be one of: robust across contexts, conditional,
mixed, weakly supported, unresolved, or not enough evidence.

## Evidence audit

| Claim ID | Exact claim | Source and evidence location | Source type/tier | Fit to claim | Independent corroboration | Limitations or risk | Status | Revision needed |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|

Use a precise location when available: page, section, table, figure, paragraph,
timestamp, dataset record, or official notice clause. If the source cannot be
opened, record that explicitly.

## Research opportunity brief

For each recommended opportunity, use this compact structure:

```text
Opportunity:
Why now / why unresolved:
Gap type(s):
Research question:
Population, context, or unit:
Mechanism, comparison, or process of interest:
Outcome or contribution:
Evidence/design route:
Main rival explanation:
Feasibility and ethical constraints:
What would count as a meaningful result:
Confidence in recommendation:
```

## Default synthesis pattern

```text
Question and scope
Evidence boundary and assumptions
What the literature agrees on
Where it conflicts or remains silent
Why the differences may exist
Strongest challenge to the leading interpretation
Most defensible conclusion
Prioritized research opportunities
Verification and next-step plan
```
